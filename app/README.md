# Moli Trader – paper trading that learns (Android)

Runs the trading algorithm on your phone: paper trades Nifty stocks with live prices and learns from its mistakes. No real money, no broker.

**Install:** download [`PaperTrader.apk`](PaperTrader.apk) (installs as "Moli Trader") on the phone → open it → allow "install unknown apps".

## How it looks and feels
Same design as the Moli finance guide (branch `claude/android-finance-app-ik79e5`): warm cream cards with hairline borders, serif headings, small-caps labels, clay / sage / ochre accents, and **Moli** – the little animated advisor who blinks, waves, thinks and frowns depending on the advice.

- **Home:** greeting, *Today, wrapped* banner, paper-account card with a profit/loss chart, Invested / Cash / Profit tiles, a *Moli says* tip (tap *Next tip*), market clock with pause/stop, positions, your stocks.
- **Activity:** charts (account vs the Nifty, drop from the peak, month-by-month return, each closed trade, profit by stock), then every buy and sell by month, with why it was sold and what went wrong on losers.
- **Train:** train on the past – watch one run or repeat until best (step by step, evolution, walk forward). Charts: account vs the Nifty while it runs; drop from the peak, month by month and each trade when done; for repeat runs, best vs current settings over the whole period with the unseen part shaded.
- **Guide:** me vs the Nifty, where your money is, **Suggestions** (heads up / idea / nice – e.g. lagging the Nifty, idle cash, a stock nothing works on, sector concentration, costs eating profits, most common mistake, drawdown, time to re-test settings), each with a one-tap action; then what the brain learned and its strategy per stock.
- **Stock page** (tap any stock): candlestick chart of the last 80 sessions with 20/50-day averages, support/resistance levels, candle-pattern markers and a 2-week trend cone; *Moli's read*; trend projection; support & resistance; today's candlestick, chart-pattern and trend signals.
- **Predictions:** 8 candlestick patterns (doji, hammer, hanging man, shooting star, bullish/bearish engulfing, morning/evening star), chart patterns (20-day breakout/breakdown, sitting on support, under resistance, double bottom/top, squeeze, golden/death cross) and trend/momentum signals (strong up/down trend, RSI extremes, MACD turns). Each is scored on that stock's own last ~5 years: how often the price was higher 5 and 20 sessions later vs a normal day, with sample sizes. Signals combine into a lean (up / down / no clear edge). When a pattern's textbook meaning didn't hold on that stock, Moli says so. Also in the Guide (*Predictions*) and the chat (*predict RELIANCE*, *predictions*). Odds are in-sample and most patterns are close to a coin flip – shown, not hidden.
- **Uptrend or downtrend?** (stock page, Guide list, chat: *trends*, *which stocks are in an uptrend*, *trend TCS*): the trend now – uptrend, downtrend or sideways, from the 50-day average's direction over 20 sessions and where the price sits – with how long it has lasted and an 80-session trend strip. A forecast for the next 20 sessions: the chance the price is higher, from a small logistic model trained on that stock's own past (5/20/60-day moves, distance from the 20/50/200-day averages, 50-day slope, RSI, MACD, volatility, the Nifty's trend), with its biggest factors. *What happened next before*: how often this kind of trend became an uptrend, sideways or downtrend 20 sessions later. *Honest score*: the model is tested walk-forward on past days it never trained on and compared with always guessing the usual direction. In tests across 10 big stocks it was right about 53% vs 53% (2021–26) and 51% vs 50% (2008–12) – so the app says when it is no better than guessing.
- **Trend calls in Train on the past:** during a watched training run, every 20 sessions Moli calls each stock's next 20 sessions (uptrend/downtrend/sideways now, chance higher, leans up/down/unclear) with a model refitted only on what was known that day. The Train screen shows the trend on the replay date and reveals each call's result only when the replay reaches it, with a running score vs always guessing; the finished run, the training report and the past-runs list show the final score. Test: 2008–10 right 61% vs 60% guessing; 2023–26 49% vs 51%.
- **Trend calls and chart predictions in Repeat until best** (step by step, evolution, walk forward): the same 20-session trend calls are made for the period (they don't depend on the settings being tuned) and scored when the search ends – overall, and split into the tuning part vs the unseen part (or the traded stretches for walk forward). The chart-predictions slider works there too. The score is added to the report and to the past-runs list.
- **Chart predictions in Train on the past:** pick a stock in a watched run and tap *Read the chart on <date>* (or, when the run ends, drag the date slider to any day). Moli draws the candlestick chart, 20/50-day averages, support/resistance, candle-pattern marks and the 2-week trend cone, and gives its read, trend and cone – all using only prices up to that day. As the replay moves on, a black line shows what really happened next, with whether it stayed inside the cone and whether the lean was right.
- **Today, wrapped:** a story recap of the trading day ending with *Moli's note for tomorrow*.
- **+ button:** ask Moli (chat), update, add a stock, change money, train, switch quick/normal, pause.
- **Settings (⚙):** name, stocks, reminder, paper money, trading style, brain settings, data downloads – including **Brain data** (JSON: settings, rules with evidence, strategy per stock, predictions, training runs, history) and **Brain evidence** (CSV of every trade the brain learned from) – and **Import brain data**: load a brain file (preview first; choose to use its settings and/or add its learned trades, deduplicated and validated) or restore a full backup (e.g. after a reinstall).

## How it works
- **Data:** free daily prices from Yahoo Finance (`query1.finance.yahoo.com`), fetched when you open the app or tap Update. Missed days are caught up one session at a time.
- **Fills:** signal at a session's close → simulated fill at the next session's open, with STT, stamp duty, GST, exchange/SEBI fees, DP charges and 0.05% slippage. Dividends are credited; splits/bonuses adjust positions.
- **Brain** (`web/engine.js`):
  - 7 strategies (buy & hold, SMA/EMA cross, MACD, Donchian breakout, RSI dip-buy, Bollinger reversion) run in hidden "shadow" paper accounts per stock.
  - Every 21 sessions each stock switches to the strategy with the best trailing-year Sharpe (≥ 0.3), or to cash if none qualifies.
  - Every closed trade is tagged with its entry conditions. A condition whose trades lose clearly more than the rest (≥ 12 trades, negative average, ≥ 1 point worse) becomes a rule that blocks new entries. Rules are dropped again if the evidence fades.
  - Before paper trading starts it practises on the previous 2 years (configurable), so it begins with learned state. Practice results are shown separately.
- **Controls:** *Pause new buys* (positions keep their stops and exits), *Stop & sell all* (everything sells at the next open), *Resume*. The Update button turns into **Stop** while downloading, with a progress bar and time estimate.
- **Stocks:** add or remove from a searchable NIFTY 50 + ETF list, or any NSE symbol (checked on Yahoo first). A new stock gets the same paper capital as the others and its strategies warm up on past data; a removed stock is sold at the next open and its cash is withdrawn. Returns are time-weighted, so adding or removing stocks doesn't count as profit or loss.
- **Train on the past** (Train tab): a separate practice account trades any past period from 2008 onward (presets: last 1/3/5/10 years, COVID crash, 2008 crisis, or custom dates) day by day, never seeing the future. Watch the replay at 1×–64× speed with play/pause, skip and stop. Choose how much paper money the run uses (default: your account size; or say *train on the 2008 crisis with 10 lakh*). Start from a fresh brain or with only the lessons from trades that ended before the period (an honest test). Afterwards, preview which rules would change and optionally **teach the live brain**: the period's trades join its evidence pool (deduplicated). Each run is saved to the history and can be exported.
- **Paper money:** add money, withdraw money (from cash only) or set the account to an exact value – in Data & settings or by saying *add 1 lakh*, *withdraw 50,000*, *set money to 10 lakh*. Deposits are split equally across stocks and new buys are sized from the new amount; all changes are recorded as capital flows, so they never show up as profit or loss.
- **Apply what it learned:** after any training run, tap *Apply what it learned* or say *apply* – the run's trades join the live brain's evidence (you see which rules would change first). Optional switch: apply automatically.
- **Repeat until best** (Train on the past → Mode): re-runs the same period up to 10–50 rounds, changing one brain setting at a time (stop-loss, how good a strategy must be to trust it, review frequency, judging window, learned rules on/off and strictness, which strategies are allowed) and keeping whatever makes more profit, until nothing improves. It tunes on the first 70% of the period and then tests the winner on the last 30%, which the search never saw. If the new settings do worse there, Moli calls it overfitting and won't apply them unless you say *apply anyway*. *Reset brain* returns to the defaults. Chat: *train last 5 years until max profit*, *30 rounds*.
- **Learning routes** for repeat mode: *Step by step* (one change at a time), *Evolution* (a population of very different settings; the fittest survive, mix and mutate) and *Walk forward* (learn on the past, trade the next unseen stretch, repeat – every result is out-of-sample). Chat: *evolve on the 2008 crisis*, *walk forward last 5 years*.
- **Quick mode** (Data & settings → Trading style, or say *quick mode*): each quick strategy is judged by its average net profit per trade over its last 20 trades, and every quick strategy averaging more than +0.3% a trade on a stock may trigger an entry. Defaults (no fixed target, 3× ATR stop, 10-session limit) were picked because they held up in both 2008–11 and 2023–26. Enters fast on sharp RSI-2 dips, pullbacks to the 20-day EMA and volume breakouts, and exits fast at a profit target (× ATR), a tight stop-loss or a time limit – trades last days, not months. The brain learns which quick strategy suits each stock and which entries keep losing; repeat training can tune the target and time limit (*train quick last 5 years until max profit*). More trades mean more STT and stamp duty, which can eat small profits – in tests quick mode won more often but earned less than riding trends.
- **Mistake memory** (on by default; Settings → *Never repeat a mistake*): when a trade loses more than 1%, Moli notes its fingerprint – the stock, the strategy and the warning signs present at entry (e.g. Nifty below its 200-day average) – and never takes that same setup again, live or in Train on the past. Training runs started with earlier lessons inherit mistakes noted before the period; *Apply what it learned* and brain import/export carry them over. Moli keeps watching what the skipped trades would have done: if a noted setup would have won more often than it lost (at least twice), it is forgiven. See them in the Guide (*Mistakes I won't repeat*) or ask *what mistakes did you make?*. In tests: 0 repeated mistakes; returns moved −0.6 to +2.3 points – small, and the skipped trades were close to a coin flip.
- **Momentum rotation account** (Home → *Momentum rotation account*, or say *momentum*): a second paper account that runs alongside your stocks. Each month it ranks the Nifty 50 by past-year return (skipping the latest month), holds the top 10 weighted by inverse volatility (calmer stocks get more), keeps holdings while they stay in the top 20 (less churn and tax), avoids selling a winner that turns long-term within 45 days, and goes to cash when the Nifty is 5% below its 200-day average. Costs, dividends, splits and capital-gains tax (STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh, settled each financial year) are applied. Downloads the Nifty 50 at each update (switch off in its settings); shows a 3-year practice run, holdings, the current ranking, trades, tax and a CSV export. Train on the past compares against it by default – watched runs (a line on the replay chart and a row in the comparison card), repeat runs (whole-period chart and an *unseen part* row) and walk forward (traded stretches); the ~80-stock download is kept in memory and reused by later runs that fit inside it, and can be switched off in the training setup.
  - **Honest test** (`node app/tests/rotation_backtest.js <dir> download`): only stocks that were **actually in the Nifty 50 on each date** can be picked – membership 2008–2025 from [vishalvx/nifty-indices-datasets](https://github.com/vishalvx/nifty-indices-datasets) (MIT, reconstructed from NSE press releases; approximate, some dates a month off; after Aug 2025 today's list). Includes members that later collapsed (Yes Bank, Suzlon, Unitech, RCOM, Idea…); a few delisted ones have no price data (HDFC Ltd, Satyam, Ranbaxy, Cairn, Reliance Capital). Benchmark: NIFTYBEES (the Nifty with dividends). Costs, slippage by liquidity (0.05–0.4%) and capital-gains tax at each year's rates (STCG 15% → 20% from 23 Jul 2024; LTCG 0% before Apr 2018, then 10% above ₹1 lakh, 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh from 23 Jul 2024).
  - **Result – it did not beat the index:** 2010–2019 (settings chosen here) +9.5%/yr vs +9.4%; 2020–2026 holdout +7.8%/yr vs +12.3%; 2010–2026 +8.6%/yr vs +10.6%, worst fall −32%. An earlier version that used "the 50 most-traded stocks" as a stand-in for membership showed +12.2%/yr – that edge was an artifact. Top-5 looked great on 2010–19 (+14.3%/yr) and failed on 2020–26 (+6.0%) – overfitting. Volatility targeting and a gradual 50% step didn't help. On the Nifty Midcap 50 (membership from 2019), simply holding every member (+19.8%/yr, 2020–26) beat momentum top 10 (+15.8%).
  - Holding **every Nifty 50 member equally** made +11.5%/yr 2010–2026 (lost in 2010–19 at +8.1%, won in 2020–26 at +16.5%); a 5% crash filter cut that to ~+7.4%.
- **Index-fund benchmark & scorecard** (Home, Momentum page, Activity chart): NIFTYBEES and a 50% NIFTYBEES + 50% Nifty 200 Momentum 30 ETF mix (from Aug 2022) are downloaded at each update. The scorecard compares your account and the Momentum account with the index fund over the same period, says "too early" before 6 months, and – if still behind at 12 months – that the honest answer is to buy the index fund.
- **What 5 years could look like** (Guide, Momentum page): block bootstrap of 2010–2026 monthly returns from the honest backtests (12-month blocks, 2,000 futures, the same blocks for every line): ₹1 lakh → momentum median ₹1.58 lakh (beats the index fund 43% of the time), equal-weight Nifty 50 ₹1.74 lakh (66%), index fund ₹1.68 lakh.
- **Live prediction scorecard** (Guide): every update logs each stock's read, trend call, indicator call and 2-week cone; they are scored when the outcome arrives (5/10/20 sessions) against always guessing, and marked "noise so far" until they do better.
- **Live fills:** during market hours (9:15 AM–3:30 PM IST, weekdays) an update fills queued orders of the paper account and the Momentum account straight away at the live Yahoo price (~1 minute old) plus the usual slippage and costs, instead of waiting for the next open – or tap *Buy now at live price* / *Trade now at live prices*, or say *buy now*. Outside market hours orders still fill at the next open. A new account's first orders are placed at the latest completed close, so it can start the same day.
- **Rebalance alerts:** when the Momentum account will trade at the next open, Home shows the orders, Moli says so in chat and the phone gets a notification – so you could copy them on your broker yourself (Moli never trades real money).
- **Beat the Nifty** (default trading style; Settings → Trading style, or say *beat the nifty*): stays invested in every stock – buying trends already under way, no stop-losses, no strategy switching. The crash filter (step aside when the Nifty is 5/8/12% below its 200-day average) is **off by default** since the honest re-test showed it cost ~3–4%/yr; existing accounts were switched once with a chat note. The numbers below come from the earlier 8-stock test, which today's-winner stocks flatter: Why: tested on 9 two-year periods 2008–2026 with 8 large caps, the old strategy-switching brain beat the Nifty in 3 (−8.1 pts on average) – it sat in cash and whipsawed in bull markets – while this beat it in 6 (+10.4 pts on average). It still trailed the Nifty in 2012–13 and 2023–26 and fell up to 23% in 2008; plain buy & hold of the same stocks did +9.7 pts on average but fell 55% in 2008. (Yahoo's Nifty history starts in Sept 2007, so until 200 days exist the average of the available days is used.) The 8 test stocks are today's winners, which flatters every result. Existing accounts are switched once (say *normal mode* / *quick mode* to go back).
- **New strategies and indicators:** volume trend (OBV), 55-day breakout on volume, signal score, trend hold (above the 200-day average); *join trends* (buy a trend already under way instead of waiting for a fresh signal) and *idle money* options for the brain.
- **Indicator call** (stock page): Buy / Hold / Sell from 5 checks – price above 50-day average, 50-day above 200-day, MACD above signal, RSI 50–75, OBV rising – with an 80-session call strip and how each call did on that stock over the next 20 sessions (it often works backwards on big stocks, and the app says so).
- **Volume chart** under every candlestick chart: up/down-day volume bars, 20-day average volume and OBV.
- **Chart predictions for the paper account** (Activity): pick a stock and slide to any of the last 250 sessions – Moli's read, indicator call, trend and 2-week cone as they were that day, then what really happened.
- **Beat the Nifty in Train on the past:** every watched run, repeat run and walk-forward run also runs Beat the Nifty on the same period, stocks and money. The replay chart shows it as a third line; the finished run gets a *vs Beat the Nifty* card (return and worst drop for the run, Beat the Nifty and the Nifty); repeat runs show it in the whole-period chart and on the unseen part; reports and the past-runs list include it.
- **Countdowns:** market open/close timer, time until queued orders fill, sessions until the next strategy review (NSE holidays not included).
- **Download data** (More tab): trades, daily equity, brain log, update history (CSV) and a full JSON backup, saved to `Downloads/Moli/` with the export time in each file name and timestamps inside.
- **Storage:** everything stays on the phone (`files/state.json`).
- **Reminder:** optional weekday 4:15 PM notification.

The engine mirrors `algo/engine.py`; on the same data it matches the Python backtester's results.

## Build
```bash
app/android/build_apk.sh     # JDK 17+, Android SDK platform 34 + build-tools 34.0.0
```
The signing key lives in `app/android/keystore/` (not committed). Updates must be signed with the same key.
